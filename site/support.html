Quick answers

Starting a procedure: Procedures tab, tap the procedure, adjust if needed, Start procedure. Mistake? Activity, History, the procedure, Edit usage or Cancel procedure.

Receiving a delivery: Home, Receive delivery, photograph the invoice, confirm each line, Confirm & receive. No invoice? Receive manually.

Inviting the team: Account, Team, Invite by email, pick Admin or Staff. The invitation is valid for 7 days.

Billing: Account, Billing. The trial lasts 3 months from the day the clinic was created; add a payment method on the secure page before it ends to keep writing.
