What it does

Home: what needs you today

To buy, Expiring soon, Missing expiry, inventory value and value at risk. No feed, no charts.

To buy: a shopping list, not a purchasing module

Fills itself from minimums and targets. Mark as ordered. Receiving the invoice closes the loop. You keep your supplier.

Bottles and syringes, handled honestly

Unopened plus open items, with who opened which. No percentage guesses.

Two roles, not an org chart

Admin and Staff. Staff see fewer buttons, never disabled ones. Invite by email.

Mistakes are fixed, never blocked

Edit usage or cancel a procedure from history with a note. Count stock from the same list, review corrections, apply.

Everything is auditable

Every change carries who, when and why. Nothing is hard-deleted.

What it does not do

No barcodes and no scanning. Consumption comes from the procedure, receiving from the invoice photo.

No patient or appointment data, anywhere. There is no field for it.

No ordering through the app and no supplier integrations. You keep ordering the way you do today.

No Android. iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 or later. Staff can use their own iPhones.
