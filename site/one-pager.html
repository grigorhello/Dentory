MediSave

Know what is running low and what is expiring. Without counting.

An iPhone and iPad app for small dental clinics, built in Oakville.

How it works

  1. The assistant taps the procedure she is about to do. Supplies come off the shelf in the app.
  2. A delivery arrives. Photograph the invoice. The app reads it and you confirm each line.
  3. Expiring lots, low stock and the shopping list show up on Home, by themselves.
